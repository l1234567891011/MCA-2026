Week 1 - Basics of Music Data

Task one : My Dataset Theme

My theme for this dataset will be the song I Need U by BTS. BTS are a 7 member South-Korean boy band who debuted in 2013 with their single album "2 Cool 4 Skool". The song was released as the title track to their EP "The Most Beautiful Moment in Life Pt.1" in 2015, and also featured on their compilation album "The Most Beautiful Moment in Life: Young Forever" in 2016. I chose this song as the theme for this dataset as I am a fan of the group and found that analysing a piece of their music would be interesting and engaging.

Task two : Challenges Faced

Working with music and music data can be challenging mainly due to access and distribution, whether this be access to recordings of music or notated sheet music, most are not available to the public and are kept behind a paywall. My selected theme displays the issue of access and distribution due to the struggle to find sheet music. Most online came from cover artists, were behind a paywall or largely kept on musescore.

Task Three : Manifestations of Data

Descriptive Data

Their company, BigHit Music, displays information about the group and their music on their website. There is a dedicated tab for their discography, where you can find information about each album they've released from the tracklists, concept photos and music videos. The website also offers a small description of each album.

Notated Data

There is a lack of official published music scores and most that can be found are fanmade, often behind paywalls.

Acoustic Data

BTS's discography can be found on streaming services such as Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music and SoundCloud.
