## My Dataset Theme

My theme for this dataset will be the song I Need U by BTS. BTS are a 7 member South-Korean boy band who debuted in 2013 with their single album "2 Cool 4 Skool". The song was released as the title track to their EP "The Most Beautiful Moment in Life Pt.1" in 2015, and also featured on their compilation album "The Most Beautiful Moment in Life: Young Forever" in 2016. ## Challenges Faced 
Working with music and music data can be challenging mainly due to access and distribution, whether this be access to recordings of music or notated sheet music, most are not available to the public and are kept behind a paywall.
